BEFORE YOU SIGN UP

Questions worth answering honestly

Do I need a credit card to try it?

No. The trial runs for one month with full access, and no credit card is required to start it.

How do I add my team?

You add employees directly in the Manage section with their role, department, and reporting manager — there's no import file to prepare first.

Is screenshot and activity tracking mandatory?

No. Screenshots and real-time activity are separate modules controlled through the Permissions module, so you decide which roles can see them.

Can employees see their own leave balance and payslips?

Yes. Employees can check their leave balance and payslips from their own account without going through HR each time.

What about access and data visibility?

Access is controlled by the roles and permissions you set up. Not everyone sees everything by default — you decide who sees what.

What if I get stuck setting something up?

Raise a ticket from the Helpdesk section inside the product and describe what's happening.
