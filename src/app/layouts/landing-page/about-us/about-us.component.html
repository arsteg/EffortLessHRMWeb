The same hours, entered three times

Most growing teams end up with attendance in one tool, leave requests approved over chat or email, and payroll prepared by hand in a spreadsheet at the end of the month. Each step is small on its own — until someone has to reconcile all three before payday.

  • A remote or field employee's hours live in a time tracker, but HR has no record of when they actually worked.
  • A leave request gets approved in a chat message, then someone has to remember to update the leave balance and tell payroll separately.
  • Payroll week means pulling attendance, overtime, and deductions from different places before the numbers add up.

EffortlessHRM keeps attendance, leave, expenses, and payroll preparation in the same system, so the hours you track are the hours your HR records already show.
