ATTENDANCE & TIME TRACKING

Attendance that fills itself in

Employees clock in through the desktop or web tracker, and EffortlessHRM builds the attendance record automatically — the same record HR and payroll use later, with nothing to copy across by hand.

Clock-ins become attendance records

Start and end times from the time tracker are logged automatically, so there's no separate attendance sheet to fill in and reconcile.

Built for remote and field teams

Attendance for off-site or distributed employees is tracked the same way as everyone else's, without relying on someone checking in manually.

Screenshots and activity, when you need them

Periodic screenshots and activity levels add context to tracked hours. Access to this is controlled through the Permissions module, not switched on for everyone by default.

One report instead of three

Tracked hours, attendance, and productivity show up in the same reports, instead of being pulled from separate tools before a review.
