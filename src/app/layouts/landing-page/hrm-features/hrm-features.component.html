WHAT CHANGES FOR YOUR TEAM

From a leave request to a final payslip, one system

Four everyday HR jobs, and how EffortlessHRM changes each one.

Leave approvals that don't need a spreadsheet

Set up leave categories and templates once, per department or location if you need to. A request routes through the approval chain you've configured — including multi-level approval for teams that require it — and the employee's leave balance updates on its own.

  • Leave categories and templates by department
  • Multi-level approval chains, not just a single sign-off
  • Balances update automatically as requests are approved

Payroll prep, not payroll guesswork

CTC templates define fixed and variable pay, deductions, and statutory contributions — including PF, ESI, and professional tax — once per role. The attendance already tracked earlier feeds straight into payroll runs and payslips, instead of being re-entered by hand.

  • CTC templates for fixed and variable pay
  • Built-in support for PF, ESI, and professional tax deductions
  • Payslips generated from the same attendance data

Expense approvals that reach the right manager

Define expense categories and policies for your company, and claims route automatically to the employee's reporting manager — so nobody has to chase an approval over email to find out where a claim is stuck.

  • Expense categories and policies you define
  • Routes to the employee's actual reporting manager
  • Clear status on every claim: pending, approved, or rejected

Onboarding and offboarding in the same place

Set up zones, locations, departments, and designations once. New employees get a role and a reporting manager from day one through the Permissions module. When someone leaves, the separation workflow carries through to a full and final settlement, instead of living in a separate checklist.

  • Organization hierarchy: zones, locations, departments, designations
  • Role-based access from the first day, not added later
  • Separation workflow through to full and final settlement
